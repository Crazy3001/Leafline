# Roadmap

What we plan to add to Leafline, and what we are looking at. It is a plan, not a promise, and
nothing here has a date. Ideas are welcome through the
[idea form](https://github.com/Crazy3001/Leafline/issues/new?template=idea.yml).

Everything stays inside Leafline's rules: it only reads the screen, never touches the game, never
sends input, and stays free.

## Planned

**Reading you can trust**
- Better reading over bright, busy maps, for the Quest Helper and for item pickups
- More tested screen sizes and UI sizes
- Every reading says how sure it is, and a doubtful one is left out instead of guessed
- Installers signed, so Windows stops warning about them

**Loot and profit**
- Recording what you pick up automatically, with the count for each session
- A guess at which monster dropped each item, labelled as confirmed, likely or unknown
- Loot value at NPC prices, and mesos per hour with loot and potions both counted
- Drop rates per kill, built from your own kills

**Your characters**
- Separate records for each character you play
- A history of each character: levels, maps, rates, gear and how fast you progress
- Compare two characters, or this week with last

**Training**
- Party and solo EXP shown apart, so you can see whether a party is worth it
- EXP lost to deaths shown on each session
- A nudge when a different map becomes better as you level
- Exact EXP across a jump of several levels

**Items, quests and shops**
- Quest rewards and requirements shown before you take a quest, including a reward read from an
  NPC's dialog on any map
- NPC shops beyond Maple Island and Victoria Island
- A before-and-after look at a gear change: stats, damage and EXP per hour
- Is this upgrade worth it: what a piece of gear gains you for what it costs
- Training spots that show their monsters and a map preview

**Maps and getting around**
- Hidden portals and where each one leads, on every map
- Spawn points for each monster on a map
- Routes that count travel time when ranking where to train

**Timers and alerts**
- Timers you start yourself, for bosses and buffs
- Field boss spawn times
- Level-up and rare-drop alerts, plus a daily and weekly summary

**Training details**
- A choice of averaging window for EXP per hour
- A mode that keeps one session going across maps
- Party quest mode: completion times and rewards
- Kills per hour and a count of kills per monster

**Party and social**
- Comparing party setups: which size and class mix trains best
- A way to coordinate with other players for parties and quests, without Leafline reading chat

## Looking at

- Town citizenship
- Map rankings and records built from many players' training, only if you choose to share, and
  never with your name unless you add it
- Community drop rates built from players' own kills, the same way
- Market prices and spotting underpriced listings, if a source agrees to it
- Showing what your gear is worth
- Notes on what an item sells for, kept on your PC
- A stream overlay for OBS
- Linux
- macOS
- Other languages
- Counts of how often Leafline cannot read something, shared only if you choose to, to fix
  misreads faster

## Not planned

- Anything that plays for you or makes AFK play easier: alerts for low HP, Lie Detector prompts,
  other players on the map, or EXP stopping
- Reading chat
- Reading game memory, files or network traffic
- Ads, paid features or accounts
