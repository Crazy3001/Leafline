# Roadmap

What we plan to add to Leafline, and what we are looking at. It is a plan, not a promise, and
nothing here has a date. Ideas are welcome through the
[idea form](https://github.com/Crazy3001/Leafline/issues/new?template=idea.yml).

Everything stays inside Leafline's rules: it only reads the screen, never touches the game, never
sends input, and stays free.

## Planned

**Training**
- Better map rankings: estimates that take monster HP and the number of monsters on a map into
  account, not base EXP alone
- Your own map records: EXP/hr and mesos/hr for each map by level, job and party size
- Mesos per hour after potion costs
- Level goals with time left, and your best rate on each map
- A tidier Train tab

**Items and crafting**
- Crafting: the recipes an item is used in, and the recipe that makes it
- A Crafting section
- A fuller, more reliable drop list

**Shops**
- NPC shops: where they are, what they sell and for how much
- "Sold by" on item pages

**Character**
- Job builds that players have really played, credited to their authors

## Looking at

- Recording drops automatically as you pick them up, with a drop rate per kill
- Town citizenship
- Map rankings built from many players' training
- Timers you start yourself, for bosses and buffs
- A stream overlay for OBS
- macOS
- Other languages
- Market prices
- Signed installers

## Not planned

- Anything that plays for you or makes AFK play easier: alerts for low HP, Lie Detector prompts,
  other players on the map, or EXP stopping
- Reading chat
- Reading game memory, files or network traffic
- Ads, paid features or accounts
